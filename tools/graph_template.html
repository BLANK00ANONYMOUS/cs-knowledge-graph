🎓 AI Tutor

The tutor needs the local server. In the repo folder run:

.\kg.cmd serve --open
Connecting…

Two modes

  • 📘 Learn — a bite-size lesson (fact-checked against your books), a quick check after each chunk, then a hands-on exercise the tutor reviews.
  • 🎓 Quiz — adaptive questions that set your real mastery level.

In a quiz

  1. Pick an answer — keys 1–4 work when the question is focused.
  2. Say how sure you are before you see the answer. Confident mistakes are the most valuable to catch.
  3. Explain why — type or 🎤 dictate. Explaining is what makes it stick.

Then you get a study plan from your books, and suggested mastery you can apply to the graph.
